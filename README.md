\# PharmaGuard



PharmaGuard is a pharmacy inventory and supply-chain decision-support system designed for government hospital pharmacies.



It helps pharmacy and medical stores teams import monthly consumption Excel files, normalize data, detect shortages, calculate average consumption, prepare next-month opening balances, review executive dashboards, and send important reports by email using n8n.



\---



\## Main Goals



\- Monitor medicine stock and consumption.

\- Detect critical shortages.

\- Identify overstock and redistribution opportunities.

\- Follow expiry and FEFO risks when batch/expiry data is available.

\- Generate purchase request suggestions.

\- Generate next-month opening balance from the current month closing balance.

\- Provide executive dashboards for pharmacy management.

\- Send reports to management through n8n email workflows.
